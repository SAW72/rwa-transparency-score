# RAT Score Points Program — Announcement Draft

> **Do not publish until reviewed by counsel.** This draft describes activity tracking without promising rewards.

**Status:** Draft. Not yet published. No token exists. No rewards are promised.

This announcement is not part of the CoinMarketCap build-a-thon submission.

## Announcement copy

**RAT Score is tracking points for early activity.**

If you generate scores, attest them on-chain, share them on X, or build an integration, that activity is being recorded. Points are off-chain scores only. They are not tokens, not securities, and not a promise of future value.

No token exists today. No rewards are guaranteed. If a token ever launches, points may be considered — but nothing is promised, and the conversion rate would be set only after activity is complete.

**What earns points:**

- Score generated — 5 points
- Score attested on-chain — 25 points
- Score shared on X — 15 points
- API integration built — 100 points
- Bug report or security finding — 100 to 500 points by severity

**What does not earn points:** referrals, holding, or transferring anything. There is nothing to hold.

**Dashboard:** read-only. Enter a wallet address, see your points by category and rank. No login, no sign-up, no connect-wallet button. Dashboard URL: [placeholder].

## Why this wording matters

- "May be considered" instead of "will be rewarded" avoids inducing consideration.
- No dollar values and no token estimates avoids pricing the consideration.
- No login or connect-wallet-to-earn. The dashboard is informational only.
- The announcement describes activity that is already being recorded, not a promise of future value.
